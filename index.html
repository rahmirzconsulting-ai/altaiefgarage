About Us

A Family Legacy of Trusted Auto Care

Zahrat Al Taief Auto Maintenance was founded in 1997 by Mr. Munawar Ali Siddiqui, with a passion for reliable, trustworthy automotive service — specialising in Honda vehicle maintenance. From day one, our philosophy has been simple: honest advice, quality workmanship, and treating every vehicle with the utmost care.

Through technical expertise and fair, transparent service, we earned the trust of drivers across the region — growing by word of mouth as customers returned and recommended us to family and friends. In 2005, the legacy was proudly carried forward by Mr. Zishan Munawar Siddiqui, honouring the vision and values established by his father.

As our customers’ needs grew, we expanded beyond Honda into complete multi-brand automotive care under one roof. Today, Al Taief Garage stands for decades of hands-on experience, family values, and fair pricing — earning trust through quality, every single day.
